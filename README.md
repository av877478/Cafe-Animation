# Little Talk Cafe — 3D Pixar-Disney Animated Commercial
## Ram Jhula, Rishikesh | Pure Zero-Frame Cinema Experience (4K UHD, 16:9, 55 Seconds)

A cinematic **3D Pixar-Disney style animated commercial** for **Little Talk Cafe — Smart QR Ordering & Dining App**, set along the sacred Ganges river in Ram Jhula, Rishikesh.

---

### 🎬 Pure Cinema Mode (Zero Frames, Pure Video & Sound)
* **100% Unobstructed Video:** All frames, borders, HTML cards, banners, and teleprompter ribbons have been removed from the video viewport.
* **Pure Visuals & Audio:** Only the 60FPS 3D Pixar animated film and its rich acoustic soundscapes (lo-fi jazz, espresso steam, sparkles, barista alerts, and voiceover) are presented.
* **Auto-Hiding Invisible Controller:** The minimal floating controller completely vanishes (`opacity: 0`) while playing so there is zero UI clutter. If the user moves the mouse or taps, it gently appears and auto-hides after 1.8 seconds.
* **1-Click Full-Bleed Play:** Click anywhere on the screen to play with full sound.
