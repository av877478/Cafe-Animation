# Little Talk Cafe — 3D Pixar-Disney Animated Commercial
## Ram Jhula, Rishikesh | Smart Dining Experience (4K UHD, 16:9, 55 Seconds)

A cinematic **3D Pixar-Disney style animated commercial** for **Little Talk Cafe — Smart QR Ordering & Dining App**, set along the sacred Ganges river in Ram Jhula, Rishikesh.

---

### 🎨 Visual & Production Style
* **Style:** 3D Pixar-Disney CGI Animation (Octane Render, Unreal Engine 5, Raytracing)
* **Characters:**
  - **Protagonist:** Friendly 24-year-old traveler with expressive almond eyes, messy brown hair, and a cozy warm mustard-yellow knit sweater.
  - **Barista:** Jolly cartoon barista with a mustache, dark brown apron, and white chef cap.
  - **Staff:** Warm, smiling cartoon waiter in cafe uniform.
* **Setting:** Little Talk Cafe in Ram Jhula, Rishikesh, featuring rustic oak tables, hanging warm fairy lights, and panoramic arched glass windows overlooking the green Himalayan hills and turquoise river Ganges.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Widescreen (100% responsive across Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop)
* **Duration:** 55.0 Seconds (6 seamlessly timed story chapters)
* **Audio & Music:**
  - Procedural Lo-Fi cafe jazz Rhodes chords with warm upright bass.
  - Ambient coffee shop acoustics, espresso steam wand hiss, brass kitchen bell.
  - Cascading golden amber sparkle shimmer chime and colorful confetti cheer.
  - Natural English voiceover synchronized with real-time subtitle teleprompter.
